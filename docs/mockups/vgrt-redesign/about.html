About Nancy Wagner

I've been where you are.

My connection to the women we support runs deep — shaped by my own journey through life's storms.

I grew up in a dysfunctional home that looked fine on the outside but struggled behind closed doors. I didn't realize how much I carried into adulthood until my marriage ended and everything unraveled. People-pleasing left me exhausted. Abandonment, rejection, abuse, and shame shaped my inner world. It felt like a perfect storm.

The next 30 years became a journey of learning, healing, and intentional living — discovering what I was missing and finally breaking free.

I've spent the last 30+ years on my own healing journey while helping other women step into a healthier, more whole life.

What I've learned is this...

We all carry stories that shape how we see ourselves and the world. Together, we learn how to recognize those patterns, understand our emotions, and respond in healthier ways.

You'll begin to see what is yours to carry—and what isn't—while learning how to set boundaries without losing yourself.

We gently uncover the roots of triggers and past pain, so they no longer define you.

You'll also learn how to have healthy emotions and emotional responses, instead of feeling controlled by them.

Through this journey, you'll release shame and move toward acceptance and forgiveness—both for yourself and others.

Most importantly, you'll discover who you truly are, what you value, and how to build relationships with safe, healthy people.

This is about becoming emotionally healthy, grounded, and free to live the life God created you for.

The Road to a HEALTHY YOU!

These were just a few of the things I personally had to learn and want to coach you through using our coaching programs. I have had the best counselors, coaches, mentors, colleagues and friends throughout my journey. Thus, I know the importance of community and support.
