What to Expect on the Coaching Road

Coaching, much like a road trip, is a transformative experience that doesn't offer quick fixes or magic solutions. It demands time, effort, and a genuine commitment to doing the work. If you are seeking a rescuer to solve your problems, coaching might not be the right fit. However, if you are prepared to take ownership of your life and invest in your growth and development, coaching becomes a powerful tool to accelerate your journey toward achieving your goals.

Just as a coach serves as an expert navigator for those seeking to enhance their lives; they also provide specific skills and support needed to overcome obstacles and make progress. If you find yourself stuck and unable to move forward, our coaches offer the accountability and structure necessary to achieve tangible results.

Embarking on a coaching journey is like having a reliable co-pilot who guides you to greater clarity, confidence, and direction. Through individual coaching, 3-month group coaching programs, or our ongoing BELONG support and educational group, our coaches help you chart a course towards your desired goals. Whether you are seeking personal growth or relationship coaching, our team is dedicated to ensuring you have the support and tools needed to make your journey as enriching and fulfilling as possible.

It's time to accelerate your personal growth and transform your life with VGRT coaching – the roadmap to a brighter YOU.

Meet Your Travel Companions — Our Coaches

Nancy's Photo

Founder & Coach - Nancy Wagner

Nancy Wagner is a certified life coach through Coach Training Alliance in Colorado Springs, Colorado. She has worked with women from every walk of life — from successful business owners to women in jail work-release programs and homeless shelters. Because no matter your background, feeling stuck can happen to anyone.

With over 30 years of personal growth experience and ongoing education, Nancy is passionate about helping women create authentic, lasting change. As the founder of Virtual Girlfriend Road Trip, she guides women in reclaiming the driver's seat of their lives through a practical, no-nonsense, faith-centered approach.

Nancy will laugh with you, cry with you, and most importantly, speak truth that sets you free to become who you were created to be. She is the proud mother of two daughters and loves being a Gigi to 4 grandchildren. Nancy is deeply committed to making transformational coaching accessible and supportive for every woman ready to grow.

Angela's Photo

Coach - Angela Euashka

Angela is a retired Licensed Professional Counselor and Special Education Teacher with over 20 years of experience supporting individuals with emotional and behavioral challenges. She earned her Master's in Counseling from Oakland University and her Special Education degree for the Emotionally Impaired from Eastern Michigan University.

She has worked with both victims and perpetrators of domestic abuse at HAVEN and brings extensive experience in emotional regulation, healthy boundaries, and personal growth. Since retiring from private practice, Angela continues to serve through life coaching and church ministry, co-leading GriefShare and Divorce Recovery groups.

She has a deep heart for women navigating difficult seasons and believes that when women invite God into the healing process, transformation becomes both deeper and lasting. Angela previously co-founded True Grace Counseling alongside Nancy Wagner and Sharon Killen and is honored to partner again through Virtual Girlfriend Road Trip.
